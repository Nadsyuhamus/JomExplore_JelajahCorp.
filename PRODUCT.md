# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travellers and local explorers planning practical Kuala Lumpur outings. They use JomExplore to discover suitable places, save favourites, and assemble a route that fits their interests, time, budget, location, and available transport.

## Product Purpose

JomExplore reduces fragmented trip planning by bringing personalised discovery, favourites, itinerary generation, transport choices, maps, and route handoff into one lightweight MVP. Success means a user can move from preferences to a usable day plan with minimal uncertainty.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, practical, and locally informed. The interface should feel calm and trustworthy while keeping travel planning approachable.

## Anti-references

Avoid tourism pages overloaded with promotions, generic AI dashboards, decorative technology imagery, unexplained recommendations, and interfaces that present estimates as live facts.

## Design Principles

- Make the next planning action obvious.
- Keep automatic decisions understandable and editable.
- Prefer useful travel context over decorative content.
- Clearly distinguish estimates, local prototype data, and live external routes.
- Preserve user control throughout itinerary planning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and plain language. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
